GRC: Duncan-Fama closed-form (Mohr-Coulomb)  |  Generalized Hoek-Brown via equivalent Mohr-Coulomb parameters (Hoek, Carranza-Torres & Corkum, 2002)  —  LDP: Vlachopoulos & Diederichs (2009) or Panet (1995)  —  SCC: Hoek, Kaiser & Bawden (1995) closed-form support-stiffness equations  —  FEM staging: LDP/GRC-derived ground relaxation (stress reduction) factor λ  —  Convergence-Confinement Method (axisymmetric, circular tunnel, hydrostatic in-situ stress)
Disclaimer: This tool is provided for preliminary and educational use only. Results should be independently verified by a qualified engineer before use in design or construction. This is not a substitute for professional judgment or compliance verification against the applicable project-specific code requirements. The convergence-confinement method assumes an axisymmetric circular opening under hydrostatic (K₀=1) in-situ stress and plane-strain conditions; it does not capture non-circular geometry, anisotropic stress, groundwater, staged excavation sequencing, or 3D face effects beyond the empirical LDP approximation used here.
