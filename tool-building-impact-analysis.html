Building Geometry & Position
The assessment is then made on the sub-surface trough at foundation level (Mair, Taylor & Bracegirdle 1993), with the equivalent beam height H = zb + height above ground.
Building Material Stiffness
Guideline (Burland & Wroth 1974; Mair, Taylor & Burland 1996): E/G = 2.6 for load-bearing masonry (isotropic, ν = 0.3). For reinforced-concrete framed buildings on shallow foundations, which are more flexible in shear, E/G = 12.5 is more appropriate (Sec. 4.5).
